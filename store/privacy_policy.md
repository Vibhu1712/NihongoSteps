# Privacy Policy — Nihongo Steps

_Last updated: <date>_

Nihongo Steps ("the app") is developed by <your name / developer name>.

**The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.**

- The app has no internet permission and never connects to the internet.
- There are no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- Your learning progress (XP, streak, completed lessons, practised characters and
  settings) is stored only on your device. If you use Android's backup feature, this
  data may be included in your personal device backup, which is handled by Google
  under your own Google account settings.
- Pronunciation uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device.
  Text is processed by that engine according to its own privacy terms.
- Handwriting you draw is compared on-device and is never saved or sent anywhere.

You can erase all progress at any time via **Me → Reset progress**, or by clearing the
app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children: the app contains no ads, purchases or data collection and is suitable for
all ages.
