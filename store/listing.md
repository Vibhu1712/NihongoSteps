## Full description (max 4000)
Start Japanese the way Japanese children do: one square of practice paper at a time.

Nihongo Steps takes you from your very first あ to building real sentences, with clear
English explanations, handwriting practice and quick games that make revision fun.
Everything works offline — no account, no ads, no tracking.

LEARN
• Hiragana and katakana charts (basic and voiced) with audio and stroke counts
• 16 beginner grammar lessons: は/です, か, の, を, に/へ, で, ます verbs (present, past,
  negative), い- and な-adjectives, 好き, あります/います, たい, てください and more
• Every lesson has examples with romaji, a "watch out" tip and a quick check
• 120+ everyday words in 10 topics — greetings, numbers, food, places, time, verbs…

WRITE
• Trace and write every kana plus 39 starter kanji on a genkōyōshi practice square
• Toggle the guide on and off, undo strokes, and get an instant similarity score

PLAY
• Kana Sprint — how fast can you read?
• Word Quiz — English→Japanese, Japanese→English or listening mode
• Memory Match — flip cards to pair kana and words
• Sentence Builder — translate English into Japanese by arranging word tiles

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• XP, levels, daily goal ring and streaks
• Romaji can be switched off when you're ready to read kana on your own
• Adjustable speech speed for pronunciation (uses your phone's Japanese voice)

Perfect for travellers, anime and manga fans, JLPT N5 beginners and anyone curious
about Japanese.
